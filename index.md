**What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?**  
The most interesting topic covered in my domain this quarter was learning about the patient pathway: how patients learn about clinics and decide which clinic to put their trust in. Choosing a fertility clinic is a very emotional and important decision, so learning how many resources people use to share their experiences and read reviews has been very interesting. There are websites like FertilityIQ designed specifically for reviews of fertility clinics and even individual doctors, which shows just how much time people put into researching this topic. This was also apparent at the clinic itself, where the care they put into the clinic's identity and the comfort and trust they provide to their patients is everything.

**Describe a potential investigation you would like to pursue for your Quarter 2 Project.**  
Something I would like to explore in our Quarter 2 project is how fertility clinics market themselves differently in order to gain people's trust and get them to choose their care over others. In this project, we are just trying to identify bottlenecks, improve the clinic's response, and see where they fall flat for the patient. Actually getting the patient through the door is something I think would be very cool to explore, and it could make a large impact for the clinic.

**What is a potential change you'd make to the approach taken in your current Quarter 1 Project?**  
One issue we encountered throughout our Quarter 1 project was collecting data on clinics other than RPSD, and we really haven't been able to make any headway. There have been many legal and permission issues with getting APIs to collect reviews and discussions from other sites. I wish we had pivoted to another method rather than spending so much time searching for ways around this issue, as it ultimately led nowhere, and now we are left with data for only the clinic we are working with.

**What other techniques would you be interested in using in your project?**  
- **Aspect-based sentiment analysis** to score sentiment for specific parts of the patient experience, not just an overall star rating.  
- **Topic modeling** (e.g., BERTopic with sentence embeddings) to discover review themes without defining them by hand.  
- **LLM-based classification** with a small hand-labeled validation set to check accuracy against human judgment.  
- **Time-series / change-point analysis** to see whether sentiment shifts after events like staff changes or new services.  
- **Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)** to test how an AI's description of the clinic changes when it is given different sets of reviews as context.
